Research

  • Proof-of-Useful-Bandwidth Blockchains
    Final Year Project, advised by Assoc. Prof. Haifeng Yu · May 2026 – Present

    Designing dynamic cut-off thresholds for a bandwidth-based blockchain secure against persistent adversaries.

  • Entropic Nearly Linear Freiman–Ruzsa Theorem (ENLFR)
    Undergraduate research, advised by Assoc. Prof. Divesh Aggarwal · Aug 2025 – May 2026

    Formulated ENLFR and proved its equivalence to Entropic PFR, extending the 2023 Gowers–Green–Manners–Tao resolution of PFR. Proved a structural lower bound via an explicit counterexample construction.

  • Cross-Linguistic Disparities in LLMs
    NUS Deep Learning Lab · Aug 2024 – Apr 2025

    Analysed activation disparities across languages with sparse autoencoders and applied activation-aware fine-tuning to improve medium- and low-resource language performance.
